# Known limitations and supported scope

1. **PDB input only.** mmCIF files, multiple-character asym/author chain IDs outside legacy PDB representation, raw MD trajectories (XTC/DCD/TRR), and arbitrary large assembly identifiers are not natively supported. Invalid format must produce a clear error, not a plausible map.
2. **One structural model per run.** Only the first model of a PDB ensemble is computed; users can export separately chosen structures for comparisons. Multi-model B-factor export must not silently label other models.
3. **Protein-only preparation.** Glycans, cofactor/NAD/metal ions, membrane lipids, ligands, solvent and noncanonical/modified residues omitted by the standard preparation are not part of the SASA/chemistry environment. This can materially bias results for relevant systems. Such exclusions are reported. Do not assume they are chemically irrelevant.
4. **Experimental structural coverage.** Missing side-chain atoms, disordered loops, alternative atom identities and uncertain occupancies may affect surface accessibility; partial models receive warnings and are not equivalent to complete experimentally resolved structures.
5. **Assembly-dependent surfaces.** Chain selection changes the physical object analyzed: an isolated subunit and its oligomer can have legitimately different exposed regions. It is not a metadata-only transformation.
6. **Sequence correspondence boundaries.** Chain-aware matching requires strong identity coverage and a sufficiently unambiguous chain pairing; equivalent homomers can remain ambiguous under rotations or homologous repeats. Do not silently infer a unique chain mapping.
7. **Physics scope.** Ionization state uses fixed pKa approximations; pH enters the model. Ionic strength and temperature are stored but are *not* explicit screened-electrostatic or thermal simulation terms. No full APBS charge distribution, protonation microstate modeling, pH-specific structures or hydration-force model is claimed.
8. **Interpretation.** The 11 chemistry channels are compatibility descriptors, not adsorption site probability, adsorption energy, kinetics, unfolding, atomistic binding pose or material-specific affinity estimates. Combined maps are intersections/co-localization descriptions, not fitted adsorption scores.
9. **Sampling/numerics.** SASA uses a finite 200-point atom-surface quadrature; exact bitwise invariance under every rigid rotation or numerical environment is not guaranteed. Different rounding policies can affect nearly tied low-ranked candidates. The declared thresholds and numerical differences are included with the evidence.
10. **Performance and interface scope.** Tests cover specific platform/Python/Chromium combinations and stress structures; not every browser, device, network condition or protein size is tested. PDB ID lookup requires network access, while local PDB analysis can operate offline after dependency installation.
11. **Validation limits.** The 231-map oracle shares the chemistry specification and standard parser; two independently sampled SASA fixtures broaden, but do not eliminate, common-mode error risk. The 7+2 historical adsorption cases were not remeasured in this release test suite.
