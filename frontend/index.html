How to read InterfaceScout

Residue compatibility combines side-chain solvent exposure, membership in a chemistry-compatible residue class, and the availability of the required ionization state.

Li,c = Ii,c × scRSAi × fstate,i,c(pH)

Compatible residues are spatially aggregated at 6 and 9 Å. A coarse interface patch is then defined as a non-transitive 8 Å surface neighborhood around a local chemistry maximum, constrained to the same outward-facing surface.

Patches are ranked by Pareto dominance across chemistry support, accessibility, patch coherence, and orientation coherence. There is no fitted weighted sum. GNM and residue-interaction-network descriptors are contextual only and do not alter patch membership or rank.

Interpretation: high-ranking patches are plausible protein-side material-contact regions. They are not adsorption probabilities, free energies, capacities, unique orientations, or exact atomic contact maps.
