InterfaceScout publication-freeze model

InterfaceScout is a deterministic, protein-centered pre-screening and interpretation framework. It maps which exposed residue/patch motifs are chemically compatible with generalized interface chemistries. It is not an adsorption simulator.

Li,c = Ii,c × scRSAi × fstate,i,c(pH)

Within each chemistry, favorable local scores are normalized to a 0–100 residue propensity. Patch density is calculated independently at 5 and 8 Å, and the canonical multiscale-persistence score is the minimum of the two independently normalized maps.

Mi,c = 100 × min[D̃i,c(5 Å), D̃i,c(8 Å)]

Auxiliary only: APBS electrostatic potential, literature Ebase values, secondary structure, B-factor, and the 8 Å neighborhood statistic. None of these is multiplied into the primary score.

Why 5/8 Å? The pair was frozen from an adsorption-independent developmental radius-pair audit as a local-plus-extended compromise with stronger top-patch positional stability. The radii are aggregation scales, not universal intermolecular interaction cutoffs. The separate 8 Å neighborhood statistic remains descriptive only and is not multiplied into the score.

Not predicted: adsorption capacity, absolute free energy, unique adsorption orientation, adsorption-induced conformational change, or material-side porosity/transport/hydration.
